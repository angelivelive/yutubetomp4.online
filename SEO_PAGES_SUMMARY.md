## SEO Strategy

### Keyword Targeting
- **Primary Keywords:** Exact match in title, H1, and URL
- **Secondary Keywords:** Related terms in H2s and H3s
- **Long-tail Keywords:** Natural integration in body content

### Content Structure
Each page includes:
1. **Hero Section** with converter widget
2. **Main Content Section** with:
   - H2: Primary topic explanation
   - H3: How-to guide
   - H3: Features/benefits
   - H3: Additional information
3. **Semantic HTML** with proper heading hierarchy
4. **Internal Links** to main pages and terms

### On-Page SEO Elements
- ✅ Unique title tags (60-70 characters)
- ✅ Meta descriptions (150-160 characters)
- ✅ H1 tags with target keywords
- ✅ Semantic HTML5 structure
- ✅ Keyword-rich URLs
- ✅ Alt text ready for images
- ✅ Internal linking structure
